# Molded Pulp for Oji India: Segment Prioritisation and Pilot Proposal

**Independent Business Research | Jayanth Ram | IIT Hyderabad | October 2026**

An independent assessment of India's molded pulp packaging market to identify attractive customer segments and propose a practical market-entry pilot.

## Research Objective

Evaluate three Indian customer segments for molded pulp packaging and recommend which should be prioritised for a pilot:

- Electronics and e-commerce protective packaging
- Food and FMCG packaging
- Automotive and industrial components

## Key Findings

**Preliminary recommendation: Food and FMCG packaging**, with electronics and e-commerce as the alternative.

The weighted scoring model gives food and FMCG a base score of **3.30/5**, versus **3.20/5** for electronics and **2.35/5** for automotive and industrial components. The ranking changes under alternative weightings, so the recommendation remains provisional.

## Methodology

1. Reviewed public company, government, regulatory, industry-association and market-research sources.
2. Benchmarked eight competitors and alternative packaging solutions.
3. Scored three customer segments using six weighted criteria.
4. Built low, base and high market-sizing scenarios using sourced figures and labelled assumptions.
5. Conducted sensitivity analysis and developed a proposed six-month pilot with testable hypotheses and decision criteria.

## Proposed Pilot

The six-month pilot tests four hypotheses:

- Buyer willingness to pay a premium for fibre-based packaging.
- Technical performance against buyer-specified requirements.
- Commercial feasibility across import, local production and incumbent alternatives.
- Buyer commitment to a trial or letter of intent.

The proposed budget is **₹8.25 lakh**, including a 10% contingency. It is an initial planning assumption, not an approved budget or verified quotation.

## Limitations

This project is based on independent desk research. No customer interviews, verified supplier quotations or laboratory tests were conducted. Market-size sources conflict, and some model inputs are assumptions. Findings and recommendations require further validation.
